About Us

Onyame Akyedie Studio is a creative studio in Bolgatanga, Ghana. We design, print and paint for businesses, churches, schools and individuals.

Whether you need a logo, a church flyer, T-shirts for your group or a painting for your wall, we work with you from the first idea to the finished product.
